About

We are Hadeon

Mission

Democratize robotic motion with a no-code engine that makes efficient, safe, robust autonomy available to any robot, run by whoever's on the floor — no programmer required.

Vision

A world where deploying a robot is as seamless as hiring an expert. Any arm, in any cell, working fenceless alongside the people on the floor — provably safely.

The problem

The industry is stuck choosing between rigid manual programming and unpredictable AI. Neither scales.

01 · Bottleneck

Deployment Is Slow

Waypoint programming needs expensive experts and breaks when the environment shifts a centimetre.

02 · Fragility

The World Isn't Static

Faced with an unexpected obstacle, a traditional cell either crashes or freezes. Both cost the line.

03 · Certification

Black Boxes Don't Pass Audit

Probabilistic planners cannot guarantee safety, so they cannot clear strict industrial certification.
