September 2024

Hadeon forms R&D partnership with Dynamium Lab

Hadeon formed a key R&D partnership with Dynamium Lab in 2024, securing access to advanced test facilities and a talent pipeline of Atlantic Canadian roboticists.

That partnership underpins much of Hadeon's algorithm work today. Dynamium's André Gallant serves as Research Director on the high-performance algorithms behind the engine. On Hadeon's side, Nikos Doiron led the numerical-optimization work, with Thomas Duquette, an R&D developer at the lab, co-leading the collision-distance piece — together the work that would later power Hadeon's 34% Kinova Link6 cycle-time result.

For an early-stage company building a motion-planning engine, access to real test hardware and a research bench matters as much as funding — it is what turns an optimization formulation on paper into something validated against an actual robot.
