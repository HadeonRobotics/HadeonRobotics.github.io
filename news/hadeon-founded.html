August 2024

Hadeon Robotics is founded

Hadeon Robotics was officially incorporated in 2024, establishing the legal and commercial foundation for a company built around one idea: robot motion should be taught by goal, not programmed waypoint by waypoint.

The same year, Hadeon was selected for the Venn Innovation Garage, an accelerator program that provided early strategic mentorship and resources as the founding team began building the engine that would become BLAST — a deterministic, no-code motion-planning core for industrial manipulators.

Founding this early gave the team room to get the fundamentals right before chasing customers: a solver built on hard collision and kinematic constraints rather than learned behavior, with safety and determinism as first-class requirements rather than an afterthought.
