Hadeon secures $195,000 in Mitacs R&D funding

Hadeon secured $195,000 in total R&D funding from Mitacs in 2025, combining its Accelerate and Business Strategy Internship (BSI) programs, to build out the company's core development team.

The BSI grant directly funds Head of AI & Growth Mario Dandurand's research into AI-driven motion prediction for dynamic environments — the work that lets Hadeon's engine anticipate the movement of obstacles that aren't standing still, a critical piece for safe human-robot collaboration.

Non-dilutive funding like this let Hadeon grow its technical team on its own timeline, ahead of any fundraise, before the technology was ready to show — the same discipline behind the decision to validate the core solver academically before taking it to market.
